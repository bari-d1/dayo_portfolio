Who I Am

I'm a serial entrepreneur and educator based in the UK, working across technology, education, and creative media. As Co-Founder and CTO of Splita, a bill-splitting app, I lead product and technical strategy. I also founded Tutor Point, an online tutoring platform serving West African and international exam students, and have over 2,000 hours of tutoring experience. In the creative media space, I run Verse by Verse, a gospel media brand offering artist lyric breakdowns, alongside Verse by Verse Edits, a sermon clip service for UK churches. Beyond these ventures, I work on web design and community-facing projects, bringing a multi-disciplinary approach that spans software development, teaching, and music. I'm driven by building practical tools and educational experiences that make a real difference for the people who use them.
